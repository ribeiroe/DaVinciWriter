# Venue Requirements — Elsevier Information and Software Technology (IST)

**Submission Format**: PDF + source via Editorial Manager
**Template**: Elsevier article template (elsarticle)
**Page Limit**: No hard limit; typical papers 20–35 pages (single column review format)
**Column Layout**: Single column (review format)
**Font**: Elsevier template defaults
**Abstract Word Limit**: ~200–300 words (structured abstract encouraged)
**Blind Review**: Yes — double-blind
**Citation Style**: Numbered, order of appearance [1] (Elsevier numbered style)
**Keywords**: 3–6 keywords
**Supplementary Allowed**: Yes (data repository links encouraged)

## Special Requirements

1. Double-blind: anonymize all author information
2. Highlights: 3–5 bullet points (max 85 characters each)
3. CRediT author contributions required
4. Declaration of competing interests mandatory
5. Data availability statement required
6. For SLR/mapping studies: follow PRISMA or similar reporting guidelines
7. Structured abstracts encouraged (Context, Objective, Method, Results, Conclusion)
8. Replication package strongly encouraged

## Sections (typical for empirical study)

1. Abstract (structured preferred)
2. Introduction
3. Background and Related Work
4. Research Design / Method
5. Results
6. Discussion
7. Threats to Validity
8. Conclusion
9. References

## Impact Factor

~3.8 (Q1 in Software Engineering)
