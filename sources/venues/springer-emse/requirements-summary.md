# Venue Requirements — Springer Empirical Software Engineering (EMSE)

**Submission Format**: PDF + LaTeX or Word source via Editorial Manager
**Template**: Springer Nature LaTeX or Word template
**Page Limit**: No hard limit (journal); typical papers 25–45 pages (single column)
**Column Layout**: Single column
**Font**: Springer template defaults
**Abstract Word Limit**: 150–250 words
**Blind Review**: Yes — double-blind
**Citation Style**: Numbered, order of appearance [1] (Springer Nature style)
**Keywords**: 4–6 keywords
**Supplementary Allowed**: Yes (appendices, replication packages encouraged)

## Special Requirements

1. Double-blind review: anonymize author information
2. Replication package strongly encouraged (link to Zenodo, Figshare, or GitHub)
3. Pre-registration of empirical studies is welcomed
4. Registered Reports track available
5. Must follow Springer's ethical guidelines for research involving humans
6. Data availability statement required
7. Competing interests declaration required
8. For systematic literature reviews: follow PRISMA guidelines
9. Supplementary materials should be referenced in the main text

## Sections (typical for empirical study)

1. Abstract
2. Introduction
3. Background and Related Work
4. Research Method (RQs, study design, data collection, analysis)
5. Results (per RQ)
6. Discussion
7. Threats to Validity
8. Conclusion
9. References

## Impact Factor

~4.0 (Q1 in Software Engineering)
