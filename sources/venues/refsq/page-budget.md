# Page Budget — REFSQ 2027

**Venue**: REFSQ 2027 — International Working Conference on Requirements Engineering  
**Template**: Springer LNCS (single column, A4, Times New Roman 10 pt)  
**Words per page (LNCS single-column)**: ~500 words/page  
**Note**: References ARE included in the page count at REFSQ (unlike INForum/other venues)  

---

## Technical Design Paper / Scientific Evaluation Paper (15 pages incl. refs)

Target: 14 body + refs pages (1-page buffer)

| Section | Pages | Word Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structured Abstract | 0.4 | ~200 words | Exactly 4 paragraphs: Context, Question, Results, Contribution |
| Keywords | — | 4–6 terms | |
| Introduction | 1.5 | ~750 words | Motivation, problem, contributions, paper organisation |
| Background / Related Work | 2.5 | ~1,250 words | Thematic groupings; map each work to the research gap |
| Research Method / Approach | 3.0 | ~1,500 words | Design decisions, rationale, trade-offs, replication detail |
| Results / Findings | 2.5 | ~1,250 words | Data, analysis, tables/figures, baselines |
| Discussion | 2.0 | ~1,000 words | Interpretation, implications, threats to validity, limitations |
| Conclusion | 0.6 | ~300 words | Contributions restatement, future work |
| Data Availability Statement | 0.2 | ~80 words | Open science disclosure |
| References | ~1.3 | — | ~30–40 references at ~2–3 lines each |
| **Total** | **~14.0** | **~6,330 words** | |

## Key Budget Notes

- The 4-paragraph structured abstract is **mandatory** and counts toward the page limit
- References count toward the page limit — budget ~1–1.5 pages for them
- Data Availability Statement is required and counts toward the page limit
- Acknowledgements must be removed from the submitted version (double-blind)
