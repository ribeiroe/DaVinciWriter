## Template / Format

**Template family**: Springer LNCS / LNBIP (Lecture Notes in Computer Science / Business Information Processing)  
**Template source**: https://www.springer.com/gp/computer-science/lncs/conference-proceedings-guidelines

### Page Layout
- **Page size**: A4 (210 × 297 mm)
- **Columns**: Single column
- **Text area**: 122 × 193 mm
- **Top margin**: ~33 mm; **bottom**: ~52 mm; **left/right**: ~40 mm each

### Typography
- **Body font**: Times New Roman (or Times), 10 pt
- **Line spacing**: Single
- **Title**: 14 pt bold, centred
- **Author names**: 9 pt, centred (anonymised for submission — see Blind Review)
- **Section headings**: 10 pt bold, numbered (e.g., "1 Introduction")
- **Subsections**: 10 pt italic-bold
- **Abstract**: structured, 4 paragraphs, ~200–250 words max
- **Keywords**: required, placed after abstract

---

## Abstract Requirements — REFSQ-Specific (MANDATORY)

REFSQ 2027 requires a **structured abstract with exactly 4 paragraphs** in this order:

1. **Context and motivation** — situate and motivate the research
2. **Question/problem** — the specific question or problem addressed
3. **Principal ideas/results** — summary of ideas, results, approach, and methodology
4. **Contribution** — main contribution (to theory and/or practice); also state limitations of results

> This structured abstract format is a hard requirement unique to REFSQ. Non-conforming abstracts will be desk-rejected.

---

## References / Citations

- **Style**: Springer LNCS numbered style
- **In-text**: Numbered in square brackets, e.g., `[1]`, `[2,3]`
- **Reference list**: Numbered in order of first citation
- **References are included in the page count** (unlike some other venues)
- **Format examples**:
  - Journal: `Author, A., Author, B.: Title. Journal Name vol(issue), pp–pp (year)`
  - Conference: `Author, A.: Title. In: Proc. Conference Name, pp. pp–pp. Publisher, City (year)`
  - Book: `Author, A.: Book Title. Publisher, City (year)`

### Citation density norms
- Technical Design / Scientific Evaluation (15 pages): ~25–40 references typical
- Experience Reports (12 pages): ~20–30 references typical
- Vision / Research Previews (8 pages): ~15–25 references typical

---

## Blind Review

- **DOUBLE-BLIND** — new for REFSQ 2027
- Author names and affiliations must be **removed** from the submission PDF
- Self-citations must be written in third person (e.g., "In prior work, the authors showed…" → "In prior work, Smith et al. showed…")
- Do not include acknowledgements that reveal author identity in the submission
- Do not include links to personal repositories or tools that identify the authors

---

## Open Science Policy

- Authors are **encouraged** to disclose research artefacts (data, code, materials)
- Each paper must include an explicit **Data Availability Statement** (similar to an acknowledgements section) at the end
- Statement must either describe disclosed materials or justify why disclosure is not possible (e.g., IP agreements)
- Further guidance: REFSQ 2027 Open Science Policy (to be published at https://2027.refsq.org)

## Required Structure (Full Research Papers)

1. **Title** (anonymised version for submission)
2. **Abstract** — exactly 4 structured paragraphs (see above)
3. **Keywords** — after abstract
4. **Introduction** — motivation, problem, contributions, paper organisation
5. **Background / Related Work**
6. **Research Method / Approach / Design**
7. **Results / Findings**
8. **Discussion** — interpretation, threats to validity, limitations
9. **Conclusion** — contributions restatement, future work
10. **Data Availability Statement** — open science disclosure
11. **(Acknowledgements — remove for submission)**
12. **References** (included in page count)
