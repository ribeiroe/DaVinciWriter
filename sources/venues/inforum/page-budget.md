# Page Budget — INForum (Full Article, 12 pages)

**Venue**: INForum — Simpósio de Informática  
**Template**: Springer LNCS  
**Target**: 11 pages body (1-page buffer under 12-page limit)  
**References**: Excluded from page count  
**Words per page (LNCS single-column)**: ~500 words/page  

---

## Per-Section Word Targets

| Section | Pages | Word Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abstract | 0.3 | ~150 words | Max 250; mandatory |
| Keywords | — | 4–6 terms | Not counted in words |
| Introduction | 1.5 | ~750 words | Problem, contributions, organisation |
| Related Work / Background | 2.0 | ~1,000 words | Group by theme; map to gap |
| Methodology / Approach | 2.5 | ~1,250 words | Design decisions, rationale, trade-offs |
| Evaluation / Results | 2.5 | ~1,250 words | Protocol, data, findings, baselines |
| Discussion | 1.5 | ~750 words | Implications, limitations, threats |
| Conclusion | 0.7 | ~350 words | Contributions restatement, future work |
| **Total body** | **11.0** | **~5,500 words** | |

## Notes

- Communications (Comunicações): target 4 pages (~2,000 words body)
- Posters / Demos: max 2 pages (~1,000 words)
- Acknowledgements and References are not counted toward the 12-page limit
