| Type | Description | Page Limit | Language |
|---|---|---|---|

> **Publications note**: After acceptance, authors may opt for (a) full publication at the stated page limits, or (b) a 2-page summary abstract in the proceedings archive.

## Template / Format

**Template family**: Springer LNCS (Lecture Notes in Computer Science)  
**Template source**: https://www.springer.com/gp/computer-science/lncs/conference-proceedings-guidelines

### Page Layout
- **Page size**: A4 (210 × 297 mm)
- **Columns**: Single column
- **Top margin**: 60 mm (title area); 33 mm thereafter
- **Bottom margin**: 52 mm
- **Left/right margins**: 40 mm each side
- **Text area**: 122 × 193 mm

### Typography
- **Body font**: Times New Roman (or Times), 10 pt
- **Line spacing**: Single
- **Title**: 14 pt bold, centred
- **Author names**: 9 pt, centred
- **Section headings**: 10 pt bold (numbered, e.g., "1 Introduction")
- **Subsections**: 10 pt italic-bold
- **Abstract**: max 150–250 words, italic, 9 pt
- **Keywords**: 4–6 keywords, placed after abstract

### Figures and Tables
- Figures and tables must be placed near their first mention in the text
- Captions: 9 pt, centred below figures, above tables
- Do not use colour figures unless colour reproduction is confirmed

---

## References / Citations

- **Style**: Springer LNCS numbered style (Vancouver-like)
- **In-text**: Numbered in square brackets, e.g., `[1]`, `[2,3]`
- **Reference list**: Numbered in order of first citation, at end of paper
- **References are excluded from the page count** (articles only)
- **Format examples**:
  - Journal: `Author, A., Author, B.: Title. Journal Name vol(issue), pp–pp (year)`
  - Conference: `Author, A.: Title. In: Proc. Conference Name, pp. pp–pp. Publisher, City (year)`
  - Book: `Author, A.: Book Title. Publisher, City (year)`

### Citation density norms (LNCS proceedings)
- Typical range: 15–30 references for a 12-page article
- All references must be cited in the text

---

## Abstract & Keywords

- Abstract is **mandatory**; max ~250 words
- 4–6 keywords required, placed after abstract
- Abstract must summarise: problem, approach, results, and significance

---

## Blind Review

- **Not double-blind** — author names and affiliations are included in the submission
- Author self-citations are permitted, but must be written in third person to avoid bias

---

## Structure (recommended for full articles)

1. **Title, Authors, Affiliations, Abstract, Keywords**
2. **Introduction** — motivation, problem statement, contributions, paper organisation
3. **Related Work** (or Background)
4. **Methodology / Approach / System Design**
5. **Evaluation / Results** — quantitative or qualitative validation
6. **Discussion** — interpretation, implications, limitations
7. **Conclusion** — summary, contributions restatement, future work
8. **Acknowledgements** (optional, not counted toward page limit)
9. **References** (not counted toward page limit for articles)
